Sensation 360

What if your headphones could read the emotion within you?

A premium headphone concept that turns emotional signals into a live colour response — making mood part of the product experience.

The idea

Sound is emotional. The product should be too.

Sensation 360 explores a more expressive relationship between people and technology. Instead of treating headphones as a passive object, the concept gives them a visible response to the person wearing them.

Emotional cues are interpreted by onboard processing, and a liquid crystal surface translates them into colour in real time.

How it works

Sense • Interpret • Express

01

Sense

The system responds to the wearer’s emotional cues as they shift over time.

02

Interpret

Microcontrollers and algorithms process the biometric input into a simplified emotional signal.

03

Express

The liquid crystal surface changes colour, creating a live visual reflection of the wearer’s mood.

Emotion interface

A display that feels alive.

The colour system is not just decoration. It becomes the visual language of the product — a soft, shifting layer between the wearer, the music and the people around them.
