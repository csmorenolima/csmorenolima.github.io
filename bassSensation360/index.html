Overview

When the product becomes emotionally responsive.

Sensation 360 explores a deeper relationship between humans and technology. Integrated temperature sensors and microcontrollers continuously monitor the wearer's body temperature.

Algorithms interpret those biometric signals and translate them into a dynamic liquid crystal colour display, creating a personalised mood reflection in real time.

The concept

Emotion becomes part of the interface.

Instead of hiding the technology, Sensation 360 makes the sensing layer visible. The exterior display responds with changing colour, turning biometric input into an expressive product behaviour.
