Product story

A speaker shaped by nature.

Drop Mini takes the familiar geometry of a water droplet and turns it into a softer alternative to the usual rectangular portable speaker.

The rounded front gives the product its personality. From there, the body tapers backwards, reducing its visual weight and helping the speaker sit naturally on a desk, shelf or bedside table. The form is deliberately uncomplicated so the silhouette remains the part you remember.

Form & interaction

Function made simple

The rear surface remains smooth and continuous, while ports and controls are placed where they are easy to reach without interrupting the overall character of the object.

Playback controls are kept compact and visually secondary to the body, so the product stays easy to understand without becoming visually busy.

Colourways

One form, six personalities.

The silhouette stays consistent while each finish shifts the character of the speaker — from understated neutrals to brighter, more playful colours.
