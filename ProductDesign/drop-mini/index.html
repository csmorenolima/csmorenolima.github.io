Product story

Exploring how nature can shape a product.

The concept of Drop Mini began with my interest in natural forms and how their simplicity could influence an everyday object. I was particularly drawn to the water droplet because of its soft, rounded and instantly recognisable form, and used its proportions as the starting point for the design.

From there, I refined the form into a compact speaker that feels approachable, stable and portable, while keeping the silhouette as the main expression of the product.

Form & interaction

Details stay quiet until you need them.

Connections and playback controls sit on secondary surfaces, keeping the main shape clean while remaining easy to reach.

Rear shell

Keep connections out of the silhouette.

Ports are grouped at the rear so the droplet profile remains uninterrupted from the front.

Control detail

Make interaction obvious, not dominant.

Compact playback controls stay easy to find without becoming the visual focus of the product.

Colourways

Same form. Different mood.

Six finishes shift the personality of Drop Mini without changing the geometry that makes it recognisable.
