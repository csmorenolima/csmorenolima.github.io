BASS · PORTABLE AUDIO

Drop Mini

Gentle in form. Bold in sound

A compact speaker shaped like a water droplet - built to travel anywhere and deliver rich, confident audio.

Explore Drop Mini View in 3D

Overview

A speaker shaped nature.

Inspired by nature, the Drop Mini redefines portable audio. Its organic droplet silhouette offers a friendly, lightweight alternative to conventional speakers, combining a soft silicone exterior with a perfectly balanced form factor.


Compact in hand yet memorable in presence, it packs a powerful audio engine focused on deep bass, extended battery life, splash-resistant durability, and seamless touch controls.

Design principles

What the form is doing for the product.

Droplet identity

The curved silhouette gives the speaker a softer personality and makes it immediately distinctive.

Small in the hand

The compact size is central to the concept. It should feel light, portable and easy to place anywhere.

Made for everyday use

Splash resistance and a simple interface support a product that fits naturally into day-to-day life.

Product experience

Small body, full feature set.

Drop Mini is designed to feel playful and practical at the same time. The feature set supports portable listening rather than overwhelming the page with high-tech drama.

Deep bass

Enhanced low-frequency response gives the compact enclosure a fuller sound.

12-hour battery

Enough playback for everyday use without making charging feel constant.

Bluetooth 5.0

Wireless pairing keeps the experience simple and portable.

IPX5 splash resistance

Built to handle casual outdoor use and everyday splashes.

Touch controls

A minimal upper interface helps preserve the soft, clean form.

Quick charge

Less downtime makes the speaker easier to live with on the move.

Colourways

One droplet, six moods.

The rounded form stays constant, while each finish shifts the personality of the speaker.

Details

A softer product language, carried through the details.

The rear view and controls show how ports, interaction points and hardware are integrated without losing the simple, compact personality of the product.
