Overview

A compact speaker shaped by softness, movement and portability.

Unlike the more immersive, emotional direction of the Sensation 360 page, Drop Mini should feel lighter, friendlier and more everyday. The core idea is not spectacle — it is a compact object with a memorable shape.

The water-droplet silhouette helps communicate approachability and portability, while the product features focus on bass, battery, touch control, splash resistance and easy use on the go.

Design principles

What the form is doing for the product.

Droplet identity

The curved silhouette gives the speaker a softer personality and makes the concept immediately distinctive.

Small in the hand

The compact size is central to the concept. It should feel light, portable and easy to pick up, carry and place anywhere.

Made for everyday use

Splash resistance and a simple interface support the idea of a product that fits naturally into day-to-day life.

Product experience

Small body, full feature set.

Drop Mini is designed to feel playful and practical at the same time. The feature set supports portable listening rather than overwhelming the page with high-tech drama.

Details
